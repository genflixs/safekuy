DMCA & Copyright Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: January 1, 2026

MyDrive respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of our Service to do the same. This policy describes how copyright owners can report content they believe infringes their rights, in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act ("DMCA") and other applicable copyright laws.

Filing a false or bad-faith DMCA notice may result in legal liability. Please ensure you own or are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder before submitting a takedown request.

1. Reporting Infringing Content

If you believe that any content available on MyDrive infringes your copyright, or the copyright of someone you are authorized to represent, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. To be valid under the DMCA, your notice must include all of the following information:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work — A description of the original work that you claim has been infringed. If multiple works are involved, please provide a representative list.
  2. Identification of the infringing material — The exact URL(s) or location(s) on MyDrive where the allegedly infringing content can be found. We need enough information to locate the material.
  3. Your contact information — Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and a valid email address where we can reach you.
  4. A statement of good faith — A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement of accuracy — A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature — A signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.

2. How to Submit a Notice

Please send your completed DMCA notice to our designated copyright agent at the email address below. To help us process your request quickly, include the phrase "DMCA Takedown Notice" in the subject line of your email.

DMCA Agent Email: dmca@mydrive.com

You may use the following template when preparing your notice:

Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice To the Copyright Agent of MyDrive, I, [your full name], hereby state under penalty of perjury that: 1. I am the owner (or authorized agent of the owner) of the copyrighted work(s) described below. 2. The following material, located at the URL(s) listed, is infringing upon my copyrighted work: - Original work: [title / description] - Infringing URL(s): [https://...] 3. I have a good-faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law. 4. The information in this notice is accurate. Contact information: - Name: - Address: - Phone: - Email: Electronic signature: [typed name / signature] Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

3. Our Response to Valid Notices

Upon receipt of a valid DMCA notice, MyDrive will take the following actions, as appropriate under the circumstances:

  • Remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material.
  • Make a good-faith attempt to contact the party who posted or shared the material, informing them of the removal.
  • Keep a record of the notice for our internal compliance files.
  • Forward the notice to the affected party so they may respond with a counter-notification if they believe the removal was in error.

We aim to review and process valid notices promptly, typically within 1 to 3 business days of receipt. Complex cases may require additional time.

4. Counter-Notification Process

If you believe that your content was removed from MyDrive in error or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification. A valid counter-notification must include the following:

  1. Identification of the removed material — The URL(s) and a description of the content that was removed or disabled.
  2. A statement of good-faith belief — A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  3. Your contact information — Your full name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. Consent to jurisdiction — A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or, if your address is outside the United States, for any judicial district in which MyDrive may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original takedown notice or their agent.
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Send your counter-notification to dmca@mydrive.com with the subject line "DMCA Counter-Notification".

If we receive a valid counter-notification, we may restore the removed material within 10 to 14 business days, unless we first receive notice from the original complainant that they have filed a court action seeking to restrain the alleged infringer.

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and our policies, MyDrive reserves the right to terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are determined to be repeat infringers. We maintain a record of takedown notices and may restrict access for users whose content is the subject of multiple valid notices.

6. Reporting Other Infringement

For concerns that do not fall under copyright — such as trademark, patent, or other intellectual property matters — please contact us at contact@mydrive.com with a description of the issue and relevant details.
