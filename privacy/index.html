2. Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following types of cookies and local storage:

Type Purpose Duration
Preference cookie Remembers your selected theme (dark or light mode). Persistent (until cleared)
Session storage Supports session-related features during your visit. Until browser is closed
Analytics cookies Help us understand how the Service is used (only if analytics is enabled). Up to 12 months
Advertising cookies Used by third-party advertising partners to serve relevant ads (only if ads are enabled). Varies by provider

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the Service, such as theme persistence.
